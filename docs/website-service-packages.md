# Ibrahim Alli — Website & Digital Services

*Reusable sales sheet · October 2026 · Internal working copy*

These are **scope starting points, not fixed-price promises**. Confirm the deliverables, hosting, content, payment milestones and timelines in a written proposal before accepting work.

## Essential — Launch
**Who it's for:** An independent professional or a new business that needs a credible web presence.

**Suggested scope**
- A compact, responsive business or portfolio website with clear navigation.
- Essential pages or page sections, such as Home, About, Services and Contact, adjusted to the brief.
- Contact links / enquiry form using the agreed provider, and a straightforward call-to-action journey.
- Basic titles, descriptions, social sharing preview, accessibility checks and launch support.

**Clarify first:** Whether branding, writing, photography, custom email, domain, hosting and form services are supplied by the client or are quoted separately.

## Business — Grow
**Who it's for:** An established business needing richer content and clearer customer journeys.

**Suggested scope**
- Custom multi-page layouts and responsive mobile design.
- Services/products, supporting information, enquiry flows and trust-building project/customer information that the client can substantiate.
- Basic SEO setup, analytics if approved, testing, and handover guidance.
- Optional content management, booking links, blog or integrations only when confirmed in scope.

**Clarify first:** Who owns and updates content, how enquiries are delivered, and what external integrations cost.

## Custom — Build
**Who it's for:** Businesses needing e-commerce, member areas, staff tools, integrations or tailored software.

**Suggested scope**
- Workflow discovery, site map or system design and implementation plan.
- Custom screens and functional modules as individually specified.
- Payments, authentication, admin tools or automation only if explicitly scoped, with appropriate security review.
- Testing, staging review, documentation and agreed support.

**Clarify first:** Customer data handling, identity verification, integrations, ongoing infrastructure fees, privacy responsibilities, maintenance and security support. A custom system requires a separate technical scope.

## Optional add-ons
Brand / logo refinement; original copywriting; product uploads; photography; basic marketing graphics; ongoing updates; domain registration guidance; training sessions; managed hosting; content management; custom analytics. Quote each item explicitly.

## Estimating a price without underquoting
1. Confirm goal, audience, content readiness and deadline.
2. List deliverables and integration boundaries.
3. Estimate design, development, testing, revisions and handover.
4. Include external service/subscription costs separately.
5. Add a contingency for reviewed risks; present a firm quote only after scope approval.
6. Use the **client proposal template** for the full written offer.

## Suggested lead response
“Thanks for getting in touch. Tell me a little about your business, what you'd like the website to help customers do, and whether you already have your brand assets and content. I'll suggest the most practical scope and prepare a clear quotation.”
